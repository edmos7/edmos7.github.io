About me

I'm passionate about ML and Deep Learning above all, with applications in robotics and computer vision.

I'm interested in data mixtures and representations for robot learning, combining behavior cloning or video pretraining with reinforcement learning post training for robotic action, dual systems combining lower level policies with an agentic component, and implementing all of the above on hardware.

My goal is to be able to take part in cool and ambitious projects that improve over what's possible today, and to keep on learning along the way.
